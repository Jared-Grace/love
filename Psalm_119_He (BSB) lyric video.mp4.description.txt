Psalm 119:33-40 (BSB) lyric video
Psalm 119:33-40 - Berean Standard Bible
All 5 pictures in this video are public domain paintings. Each painter and painting is named in this description.
The singing was made with Mureka.

The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Carl Vilhelm Holsoe, A Mother and Child at a Window - the mother standing in her dark dress holding the baby up in both arms at the open window, the tall white window and its curtain filling the whole left side of the room, the bare winter trees showing through the glass outside, the quiet panelled wall standing plain behind them, teach me O LORD the way of Your statutes and I will keep them to the end, give me understanding that I may obey Your law
Yefim Volkov, On the Way to Pasture - the horsemen riding away down the deep rutted muddy road at dusk with one rider coming on alone behind them, the dog running out at the left of the track, the flooded ruts shining with the last of the light, the wide pale evening sky standing over the whole open country, and follow it with all my heart, direct me in the path of Your commandments for there I find delight
Francisco de Zurbaran, Still Life with Four Vessels - the cream two handled jug standing on the bare ledge with the small red bottle set beside it, the light falling clean down one side of each vessel, everything behind them black and empty and still, turn my heart to Your testimonies and not to covetous gain, turn my eyes away from worthless things, revive me with Your word
Prince Eugen Duke of Narke, The Cloud - the one great white cloud standing up tall and bright in the blue summer sky over the whole picture, the golden hillside sloping away below it with the pale track winding down through the meadow, the dark mass of trees holding the right hand side, establish Your word to Your servant to produce reverence for You, turn away the disgrace I dread for Your judgments are good
Auguste Renoir, A Girl with a Watering Can - the small girl standing on the garden path in her blue dress with the green watering can held down in one hand and a few white daisies gathered in the other, the red ribbon in her hair, the roses and the flowering hedge crowding green all round her, how I long for Your precepts, revive me in Your righteousness

33 Teach me, O LORD, the way of Your statutes, and I will keep them to the end.
34 Give me understanding that I may obey Your law, and follow it with all my heart.
35 Direct me in the path of Your commandments, for there I find delight.
36 Turn my heart to Your testimonies and not to covetous gain.
37 Turn my eyes away from worthless things; revive me with Your word.
38 Establish Your word to Your servant, to produce reverence for You.
39 Turn away the disgrace I dread, for Your judgments are good.
40 How I long for Your precepts! Revive me in Your righteousness.
