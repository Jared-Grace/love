The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Valentin Serov, Girl with Peaches - the girl sitting at the long white table in her pale pink blouse with the black bow at her breast, the ripe peaches and the green leaves laid out along the cloth in front of her and one more held under her hand, the tall sunlit window and the whitewashed wall standing bright behind her, deal bountifully with Your servant that I may live and keep Your word, open my eyes that I may see wondrous things from Your law
Vasily Perov, The Wanderer - the old man standing alone in his patched coat with the bundle tied up on his back and the rolled umbrella laid across his shoulder, the tin mug hanging at his side and both hands closed over the top of his staff, his bast shoes worn thin and the bare brown wall standing empty behind him, I am a stranger on the earth do not hide Your commandments from me, my soul is consumed with longing for Your judgments at all times
Bengt Nordenberg, A Revivalist Disturbs the Merrymaking - the old preacher standing with his arm flung right out as he rebukes them, the man in the feathered hat lifting his glass at him and holding up the great open Bible in his other hand, the young woman kneeling down on the floor with her hand raised and her head bowed low, the fiddler and the dancers at the lit window behind, you rebuke the arrogant the cursed who stray from Your commandments, remove my scorn and contempt for I have kept Your testimonies
Abram Arkhipov, Girl in Red - the young woman sitting with her arms folded in her brilliant red blouse and her flowered red kerchief, her face quiet and steady and turned a little away from them, the white apron spread out over her knees and the dark room and the pale window standing behind her, though rulers sit and slander me Your servant meditates on Your statutes, Your testimonies are indeed my delight they are my counselors

17 Deal bountifully with Your servant, that I may live and keep Your word.
18 Open my eyes that I may see wondrous things from Your law.
19 I am a stranger on the earth; do not hide Your commandments from me.
20 My soul is consumed with longing for Your judgments at all times.
21 You rebuke the arrogant—the cursed who stray from Your commandments.
22 Remove my scorn and contempt, for I have kept Your testimonies.
23 Though rulers sit and slander me, Your servant meditates on Your statutes.
24 Your testimonies are indeed my delight; they are my counselors.
