The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Albert Edelfelt, Women outside the Church at Ruokolahti - the old woman in the white headscarf sitting up against the grey stone wall of the church with her striped apron over her knees and her arms folded, the young woman in the red checked neckerchief sitting down on the grass beside her with her bundle in her lap, the other churchgoers in their white kerchiefs standing and walking along the wall behind them in the summer light, Your word O LORD is everlasting, it is firmly fixed in the heavens, Your faithfulness continues through all generations
Harald Sohlberg, Street in Roros in Winter - the old wooden houses of the mining town standing deep in the snow along the street with their roofs and fences loaded white, the church tower with its clock rising above them at the end of the way, the still grey winter sky filling the whole upper half, You established the earth and it endures, Your ordinances stand to this day, for all things are servants to You
Helene Schjerfbeck, The Convalescent - the sick child propped up in the big wicker chair in the white nightgown and the dark jacket, holding out the twig with its first green buds over the blue and white mug on the table, the bright window light falling across the chair and the plain wall behind, if Your law had not been my delight then I would have perished in my affliction, I will never forget Your precepts for by them You have revived me
Michael Ancher, Chief Lifeboatman Lars Kruse - the rescuer standing square to us in his bright yellow oilskin coat with the black buttons, the heavy black fur cap pulled down over his weathered face and his dark beard, the plain grey ground behind him, I am Yours save me for I have sought Your precepts, the wicked wait to destroy me but I will ponder Your testimonies
Peder Severin Kroyer, Summer Evening on Skagen Beach - the half moon standing high in the pale blue evening sky and its long track of light running all the way down the still water to the shore, the sailing boat lying far out at the left and the low waves breaking gold on the flat wet sand, I have seen a limit to all perfection, but Your commandment is without limit

90 Your faithfulness continues through all generations; You established the earth, and it endures.
91 Your ordinances stand to this day, for all things are servants to You.
92 If Your law had not been my delight, then I would have perished in my affliction.
93 I will never forget Your precepts, for by them You have revived me.
