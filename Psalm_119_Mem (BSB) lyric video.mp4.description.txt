Psalm 119:97-104 (BSB) lyric video
Psalm 119:97-104 - Berean Standard Bible
All 4 pictures in this video are public domain paintings. Each painter and painting is named in this description.
The singing was made with Mureka.

The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Carl Spitzweg, The Bookworm - the old scholar standing up on the library steps in his black coat and breeches with a book held open close to his face, two more books gripped under his arm and against his knee and the handkerchief hanging from his pocket, the tall shelves crowded with volumes rising on either side of him and the painted ceiling above, oh how I love Your law, all day long it is my meditation, Your commandments make me wiser than my enemies
Henry Ossawa Tanner, The Young Sabot Maker - the boy in the blue smock braced over the shaving horse working the blade through the block of wood, the grey haired master standing behind the bench with the finished clog in his hands watching the work, the floor heaped deep with pale wood chips and the window light coming in at the right, for they are always with me, I have more insight than all my teachers, for Your testimonies are my meditation, I discern more than the elders
Pinturicchio, Saint Catherine of Alexandria with a Donor - the crowned saint standing quietly in her blue gown and red mantle with the clasped book held against her side and her sword set upright on the broken wheel beside her, the kneeling donor at her right in his dark blue cloak with his hands pressed together in prayer, the gold ground behind them and the green hill and the thin trees at the edges, for I obey Your precepts, I have kept my feet from every evil path that I may keep Your word, I have not departed from Your ordinances for You Yourself have taught me
Erik Werenskiold, September - the two country girls leaning on the rail of the pole fence out on the green hillside, the elder in the brown headscarf turning to look back and the younger resting her arms on the rail and gazing away down the valley, the bright grass running out below them and the blue ridge standing along the whole horizon, how sweet are Your words to my taste, sweeter than honey in my mouth, I gain understanding from Your precepts, therefore I hate every false way

97 Oh, how I love Your law! All day long it is my meditation.
98 Your commandments make me wiser than my enemies, for they are always with me.
99 I have more insight than all my teachers, for Your testimonies are my meditation.
100 I discern more than the elders, for I obey Your precepts.
101 I have kept my feet from every evil path, that I may keep Your word.
102 I have not departed from Your ordinances, for You Yourself have taught me.
103 How sweet are Your words to my taste—sweeter than honey in my mouth!
104 I gain understanding from Your precepts; therefore I hate every false way.
