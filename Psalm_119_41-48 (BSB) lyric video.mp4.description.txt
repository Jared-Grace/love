All 4 pictures in this video are public domain paintings. Each painter and painting is named in this description.

The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Albin Egger-Lienz, Lunch - the family standing round the bare table with their heads bowed low and their hands clasped in front of them before they eat, the woman at the right in her dark bodice and white apron with her face bent down, the plain warm wall of the room standing bare and quiet above them all, may Your loving devotion come to me O LORD, Your salvation according to Your promise, then I can answer him who taunts
Frits Thaulow, Mill Scene - the dark river running free and unfrozen down the middle of the whole picture with the snow lying thick along both banks, the red brick mills standing close over the water and the little weir breaking white beneath them, the snowy trees and the pale winter sky above, for I trust in Your word, never take Your word of truth from my mouth for I hope in Your judgments, I will always obey Your law forever and ever, and I will walk in freedom
Ford Madox Brown, John Wycliffe reading his translation of the Bible to John of Gaunt - the preacher standing barefoot in his plain brown habit with the great open book held up in both hands as he reads it out to them, the listeners sitting close at either side of him, the painted gilt arch and the blue sky standing over the whole scene, for I have sought Your precepts, I will speak of Your testimonies before kings and I will not be ashamed, I delight in Your commandments because I love them
Firs Zhuravlev, Girl with a Flower Wreath - the girl lying out in the grass in her white sleeves and her red beaded necklace with the crown of daisies and cornflowers in her hair, her hand lifted up to her lips holding the little flower on its stem, her eyes turned away and her whole face still with thinking, the dark summer wood standing quiet behind her, I lift up my hands to Your commandments which I love, and I meditate on Your statutes

41 May Your loving devotion come to me, O LORD, Your salvation, according to Your promise.
42 Then I can answer him who taunts, for I trust in Your word.
43 Never take Your word of truth from my mouth, for I hope in Your judgments.
44 I will always obey Your law, forever and ever.
45 And I will walk in freedom, for I have sought Your precepts.
46 I will speak of Your testimonies before kings, and I will not be ashamed.
47 I delight in Your commandments because I love them.
48 I lift up my hands to Your commandments, which I love, and I meditate on Your statutes.
