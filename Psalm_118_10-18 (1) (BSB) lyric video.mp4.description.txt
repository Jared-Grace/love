The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:

Henry Ossawa Tanner, Daniel in the Lions' Den — hemmed in on every side, and kept
Domenichino, Landscape with Moses and the Burning Bush — thorns alight in the wilderness
Ferdinand Bol, The Captain of God's Army Appearing to Joshua — the drawn sword of the LORD's own commander
Caravaggio, The Conversion on the Way to Damascus — thrown down to the ground, and lifted by a hand not his own
William-Adolphe Bouguereau, Song of the Angels — strength and song, and the music of salvation
Deësis mosaic, Hagia Sophia, Christ Pantocrator — the right hand of the LORD raised and exalted
Giulio Clovio, King David Dancing before the Ark of the Covenant — David leaping and shouting before the LORD, alive to tell it
Pieter Lastman, Jonah and the Whale — chastened in the deep, then cast up alive on the shore

10 All the nations surrounded me, but in the name of the LORD I cut them off.
11 They surrounded me on every side, but in the name of the LORD I cut them off.
12 They swarmed around me like bees, but they were extinguished like burning thorns; in the name of the LORD I cut them off.
13 I was pushed so hard I was falling, but the LORD helped me.
14 The LORD is my strength and my song, and He has become my salvation.
15 Shouts of joy and salvation resound in the tents of the righteous: “The right hand of the LORD performs with valor!
16 The right hand of the LORD is exalted! The right hand of the LORD performs with valor!”
17 I will not die, but I will live and proclaim what the LORD has done.
18 The LORD disciplined me severely, but He has not given me over to death.
