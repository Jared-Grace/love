Psalm 119:57-64 (BSB) lyric video
Psalm 119:57-64 - Berean Standard Bible
All 5 pictures in this video are public domain paintings. Each painter and painting is named in this description.
The singing was made with Mureka.

The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Berndt Lindholm, Longing - the young woman standing still in the garden in her white dress and her white bonnet, her head bowed and her hands folded together at her breast, the dark summer leaves closing round her and one patch of blue sky showing through above, the LORD is my portion I have promised to keep Your words, I have sought Your face with all my heart
Harriet Backer, Blue Interior - the woman sitting alone at her sewing by the window with her head bent down over the work in her hands, the whole quiet room washed blue and grey around her, the gilt mirror and the little sea picture on the wall and the red bureau standing in the corner, be gracious to me according to Your promise, I considered my ways and turned my steps to Your testimonies
Joaquin Sorolla, The Fishermen with Their Nets - the barefoot fisherman striding out fast across the open ground with the heavy net heaved up over his shoulder in both hands, the long net trailing away behind him over the stones, the white sails of the drawn up boats and the bright clouded sky standing above the whole shore, I hurried without hesitating to keep Your commandments, though the ropes of the wicked bind me I do not forget Your law
Vasily Perov, Portrait of Fyodor Dostoevsky - the man sitting alone in the near dark with his hands clasped tight together on his knee and his head bent forward, one quiet light coming in from the left across his face and his hands, everything behind him brown and deep and still, at midnight I rise to give You thanks for Your righteous judgments, I am a friend to all who fear You and to those who keep Your precepts
Isaac Levitan, Golden Autumn - the birches standing up blazing yellow and gold all along the bank, the deep blue river running down through the middle of the field, the green meadow and the far woods beyond it and the wide blue sky with its white clouds over everything, the earth is filled with Your loving devotion O LORD, teach me Your statutes

57 The LORD is my portion; I have promised to keep Your words.
58 I have sought Your face with all my heart; be gracious to me according to Your promise.
59 I considered my ways and turned my steps to Your testimonies.
60 I hurried without hesitating to keep Your commandments.
61 Though the ropes of the wicked bind me, I do not forget Your law.
62 At midnight I rise to give You thanks for Your righteous judgments.
63 I am a friend to all who fear You, and to those who keep Your precepts.
64 The earth is filled with Your loving devotion, O LORD; teach me Your statutes.
