The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Vasily Tropinin, Portrait of Arseny Tropinin - the boy turned a little to one side with his tousled fair brown curls falling over his forehead and his wide dark eyes looking away, the open white shirt collar loose at his throat and the dotted waistcoat and brown jacket over his shoulders, the warm brown and olive ground standing quiet all round him, how can a young man keep his way pure, by guarding it according to Your word, with all my heart I have sought You do not let me stray from Your commandments
Johann Georg Meyer von Bremen, The Secret - the small girl in her red skirt and yellow sleeves leaning right in with her hand cupped at the ear of the young woman, the woman sitting still in her white cap and dotted apron with the knife stopped over the bowl of cabbage in her lap, the hearth glowing low behind them with the kettle hanging over it and the little oil lamp at the wall, I have hidden Your word in my heart that I might not sin against You, blessed are You O LORD teach me Your statutes, with my lips I proclaim all the judgments of Your mouth
Pekka Halonen, An Ode to Finland - the young man standing barefoot in the cut grass in his loose white shirt and blue trousers with the scythe set upright against him and the whetstone in his hand, the second mower swinging his blade through the standing hay behind him and the woman in blue raking further off, the lake and the dark treeline and the wide pale summer sky beyond, I rejoice in the way of Your testimonies as much as in all riches, I will meditate on Your precepts and regard Your ways
Gabriel Metsu, Woman Reading a Letter - the young woman sitting with the opened letter held up in both hands over the blue cushion in her lap, her coral skirt spread wide and her white cap bent down over the page, the maid standing behind with the pail on her arm pulling the green curtain back from the picture of the ships, the little dog looking up and the sewing basket and the tiled floor in the quiet daylit room, I will delight in Your statutes, I will not forget Your word

9 How can a young man keep his way pure? By guarding it according to Your word.
10 With all my heart I have sought You; do not let me stray from Your commandments.
11 I have hidden Your word in my heart that I might not sin against You.
12 Blessed are You, O LORD; teach me Your statutes.
13 With my lips I proclaim all the judgments of Your mouth.
14 I rejoice in the way of Your testimonies as much as in all riches.
15 I will meditate on Your precepts and regard Your ways.
16 I will delight in Your statutes; I will not forget Your word.
