The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Akseli Gallen-Kallela, Boy with a Crow - the small barefoot boy standing alone out in the pale dry meadow in his white shirt and his patched brown trousers, his cropped head turned in profile as he gazes away across the field with his hands hanging at his sides, the thin grass and the grey stones running out all round him, my soul faints for Your salvation I wait for Your word, my eyes fail looking for Your promise
Julius Exner, A Visit to Grandfather - the old man in the white knitted cap stooping forward with his long pipe held in his hand and his worn face bent down smiling, the barefoot girl in the grey headscarf standing at the left spinning her white wool, the dark carved cupboard and the pewter tankard behind them in the dim cottage room, I ask when will You comfort me, though I am like a wineskin dried up by smoke I do not forget Your statutes, how many days must Your servant wait
Vincent van Gogh, The Prison Courtyard - the prisoners tramping round and round in their ring at the bottom of the yard with their heads down and their caps pulled low, the one fair haired man in the middle looking straight out at us, the two keepers in their top hats standing watching at the right, the great brick walls going up on every side, the arrogant have dug pits for me in violation of Your law, all Your commandments are faithful, I am persecuted without cause help me
Eugene Burnand, Heimgefunden - the son come home bowed with his face buried against his father's breast and his arms round him, the old white bearded father holding him close with one hand laid on his head, the pale stone wall of the house standing quiet behind them, they almost wiped me from the earth but I have not forsaken Your precepts, revive me according to Your loving devotion that I may obey the testimony of Your mouth

81 My soul faints for Your salvation; I wait for Your word.
82 My eyes fail, looking for Your promise; I ask, “When will You comfort me?”
83 Though I am like a wineskin dried up by smoke, I do not forget Your statutes.
84 How many days must Your servant wait? When will You execute judgment on my persecutors?
85 The arrogant have dug pits for me in violation of Your law.
86 All Your commandments are faithful; I am persecuted without cause—help me!
87 They almost wiped me from the earth, but I have not forsaken Your precepts.
88 Revive me according to Your loving devotion, that I may obey the testimony of Your mouth.
