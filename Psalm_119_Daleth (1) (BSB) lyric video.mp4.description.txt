All 4 pictures in this video are public domain paintings. Each painter and painting is named in this description.

The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Jules Breton, The Weeders - the women kneeling right down in the stubble field weeding the ground with their hands, one seated in her white sleeves and green kerchief, one bending forward on her hands and knees, one in a blue hood reaching down into the earth, the whole burning sunset sky standing over the flat open country behind them, my soul cleaves to the dust revive me according to Your word, I recounted my ways and You answered me teach me Your statutes
Adolph Menzel, The Balcony Room - the quiet empty room with the tall white gauze curtain blowing in at the open balcony door, the sunlight laid in one long bright streak across the bare ochre floorboards, the single chair standing at the left and the dark mirror on the wall at the right, make clear to me the way of Your precepts then I will meditate on Your wonders, my soul melts with sorrow strengthen me according to Your word
Jean-Eugene Buland, Propaganda - the white haired old man sitting in the carved chair with his hands clasped quietly in his lap, the young girl's hand resting on his shoulder and the bearded man and the woman in the black bonnet standing close behind him, the canvasser's bare hand reaching in from the edge of the room to thrust the printed sheet at him over the stacked cheap prints, remove me from the path of deceit and graciously grant me Your law, I have chosen the way of truth I have set Your ordinances before me
Ralph Hedley, The Tournament - the boy carrying his rider up on his back with both hands locked tight round the legs he is holding, the rider leaning right out laughing with his cap on and his red scarf flying as he grips the other boy's hand across the open ground, the watching boys standing and sitting along the right of the field in the afternoon light, I cling to Your testimonies O LORD let me not be put to shame, I run in the path of Your commandments for You will enlarge my heart

25 My soul cleaves to the dust; revive me according to Your word.
26 I recounted my ways, and You answered me; teach me Your statutes.
27 Make clear to me the way of Your precepts; then I will meditate on Your wonders.
28 My soul melts with sorrow; strengthen me according to Your word.
29 Remove me from the path of deceit and graciously grant me Your law.
30 I have chosen the way of truth; I have set Your ordinances before me.
31 I cling to Your testimonies, O LORD; let me not be put to shame.
32 I run in the path of Your commandments, for You will enlarge my heart.
