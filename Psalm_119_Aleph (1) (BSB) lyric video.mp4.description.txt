The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Frederik Vermehren, En saedemand - the sower striding out over the ploughed brown field in his pale knitted jacket and cap with the seed bag slung across him and his hand swung wide in the throw, the whitewashed village church standing small on the horizon behind him, the pale lilac morning sky lying over the whole open country, blessed are those whose way is blameless who walk in the Law of the LORD, blessed are those who keep His testimonies and seek Him with all their heart
Ralph Hedley, The Butter Churn - the boy bent right over in his leather apron and corduroy trousers with both hands on the crank turning the great wooden barrel churn, the wooden pail set under it and his heavy boots planted on the flagged floor, the dairy table with its blue and white bowls and the latticed window standing in the light at the left, they do no iniquity they walk in His ways, You have ordained Your precepts that we should keep them diligently
Laurits Andersen Ring, The Lineman - the railwayman standing still at his post in his long blue coat and peaked cap with his wooden clogs on the gravel and his hands down at his sides, the rails running away straight and bright past the sleepers and the telegraph poles to the far off houses, the green bank and the pale grey sky holding the quiet of the morning, oh that my ways were committed to keeping Your statutes, then I would not be ashamed when I consider all Your commandments
Viggo Johansen, Merry Christmas - the lit tree standing up through the whole dark room with its candles burning all down the branches and the star at the very top, the mother in her black dress reaching her hand out to the small child who stands with both arms stretched wide to take it, the other women joining the ring in the shadow behind her, I will praise You with an upright heart when I learn Your righteous judgments, I will keep Your statutes do not utterly forsake me

1 Blessed are those whose way is blameless, who walk in the Law of the LORD.
2 Blessed are those who keep His testimonies and seek Him with all their heart.
3 They do no iniquity; they walk in His ways.
4 You have ordained Your precepts, that we should keep them diligently.
5 Oh, that my ways were committed to keeping Your statutes!
6 Then I would not be ashamed when I consider all Your commandments.
7 I will praise You with an upright heart when I learn Your righteous judgments.
8 I will keep Your statutes; do not utterly forsake me.
