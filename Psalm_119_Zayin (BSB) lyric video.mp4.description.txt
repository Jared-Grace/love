The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Karoly Ferenczy, October - the woman sitting out in the bright autumn garden under her wide straw hat with the open paper held up in both hands before her, the big pale parasol standing over her, the laid table in front with its striped cloth and the blue cups and the apples set out on it, the orchard trees and the blue sky behind, remember Your word to Your servant upon which You have given me hope, this is my comfort in affliction that Your promise has given me life
Anton von Werner, Luther at the Diet of Worms - the friar standing alone in his black habit with his right hand laid flat on his breast and his head lifted as he answers them, the seated and standing assembly crowded round him on every side watching his face, the plain pale wall of the hall standing above the whole room, the arrogant utterly deride me but I do not turn from Your law, I remember Your judgments of old O LORD and in them I find comfort, rage has taken hold of me because of the wicked who reject Your law
John Atkinson Grimshaw, Moonlight on the Lake - the moon standing small and bright in the pale green night sky with its long track of light laid down the still water, the bare trees reaching their branches across the top of the whole picture, the paling fence running down the slope to the little gate and the wet path shining up out of the dark leaves, your statutes are songs to me in the house of my pilgrimage, in the night O LORD I remember Your name that I may keep Your law
Vincent van Gogh, Weaver at the Loom - the weaver sitting in the middle of his great wooden loom in his dark jacket and cap with both hands up at the thread, the heavy beams of the frame rising round him and the finished cloth rolled out along the front of it, the pale rough wall of the cottage standing behind, this is my practice for I obey Your precepts

49 Remember Your word to Your servant, upon which You have given me hope.
50 This is my comfort in affliction, that Your promise has given me life.
51 The arrogant utterly deride me, but I do not turn from Your law.
52 I remember Your judgments of old, O LORD, and in them I find comfort.
53 Rage has taken hold of me because of the wicked who reject Your law.
54 Your statutes are songs to me in the house of my pilgrimage.
55 In the night, O LORD, I remember Your name, that I may keep Your law.
56 This is my practice, for I obey Your precepts.
