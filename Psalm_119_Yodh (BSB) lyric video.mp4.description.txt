Psalm 119:73-80 (BSB) lyric video
Psalm 119:73-80 - Berean Standard Bible
All 5 pictures in this video are public domain paintings. Each painter and painting is named in this description.
The singing was made with Mureka.

The paintings in this video, in the order they come up:
Berthe Morisot, The Cradle - the young mother sitting in her dark blue jacket with the black ribbon at her throat, her hand raised to her cheek as she looks down and her other hand resting on the rail of the cradle beside her, the baby's small pink face asleep behind the pale gauze veil, Your hands have made me and fashioned me, give me understanding to learn Your commandments
Wilhelm Marstrand, Church-Goers Arriving by Boat at Leksand - the long boats coming in across the lake packed with the parish people in their white headcloths, the church standing up on its wooded point above the water, the crowd gathered along the sand with their children in their arms and the summer clouds piled high over the whole lake, may those who fear You see me and rejoice for I have hoped in Your word, I know O LORD that Your judgments are righteous and that in faithfulness You have afflicted me
Walter Langley, Never Morning Wore To Evening But Some Heart Did Break - the young fisherman's wife bent over with her face hidden in her hands on the harbour wall, the old woman leaning down beside her with her hand laid on her shoulder and her worn face close to hers, the pale sea and the lighthouse standing quiet behind them, may Your loving devotion comfort me I pray according to Your promise to Your servant, may Your compassion come to me that I may live for Your law is my delight
Eero Jarnefelt, Under the Yoke - the young girl standing out in the ash in her torn white shirt with her face smudged dark with soot, looking straight out at us as she holds the long pole, the man behind her at the left turning the burning brushwood, the grey smoke going up over the whole cleared ground, may the arrogant be put to shame for subverting me with a lie, I will meditate on Your precepts, may those who fear You turn to me, those who know Your testimonies
Vilhelm Hammershoi, Dust Motes Dancing in the Sunbeams - the tall white window standing in the quiet grey room with the light coming through its small panes, the bars of sun falling slantwise across the panelled door and down onto the bare empty floor, nothing else in the room at all, may my heart be blameless in Your statutes that I may not be put to shame

73 Your hands have made me and fashioned me; give me understanding to learn Your commandments.
74 May those who fear You see me and rejoice, for I have hoped in Your word.
75 I know, O LORD, that Your judgments are righteous, and that in faithfulness You have afflicted me.
76 May Your loving devotion comfort me, I pray, according to Your promise to Your servant.
77 May Your compassion come to me, that I may live, for Your law is my delight.
78 May the arrogant be put to shame for subverting me with a lie; I will meditate on Your precepts.
79 May those who fear You turn to me, those who know Your testimonies.
80 May my heart be blameless in Your statutes, that I may not be put to shame.
